# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact details on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/jowinjohnchemban). Reports are handled confidentially.
